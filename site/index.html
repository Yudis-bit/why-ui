  1. Download the extension ZIP, extract it into a stable directory, and load it unpacked from chrome://extensions.
  2. Enter the pairing token in extension Options. Configure your coding agent's MCP connection.
  3. Open your app, select the why-ui toolbar action, and move the pointer over the interaction. For a fully covered target, provide its known selector.

Two tools. One loop.

inspect_interaction returns browser hits, sampled reachability, conservative stacking causality, and source references.

verify_fix observes changed source and runtime, reconciles identity, rebuilds geometry and sampling, and checks a meaningful Safe Core.

Uncertainty stays visible.

PASS, FAIL, or INCONCLUSIVE. Missing maps stay UNMAPPED; static correlations are labeled heuristic. A replacement blocker still fails verification.

Ratios are estimates. This does not prove event-handler correctness, keyboard behavior, or every point in a shape.
